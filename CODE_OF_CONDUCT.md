# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about the code, not the person writing it.

## What is expected

- Assume the other person is trying to get it right.
- Give feedback about the change, with a reason attached.
- Accept that maintainers may say no, and that a "no" is about scope, not
  about you.
- Remember that people here work in different time zones and languages, and
  that most of them are doing this in their spare time.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, sustained disruption of a
discussion, publishing someone's private information, or any conduct that
would reasonably make someone feel unwelcome for who they are rather than
what they wrote.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and
anywhere else the project is represented.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/jinsim/f1verse/security/advisories/new),
which is the repository's only private channel, or by contacting the
maintainer directly through GitHub.

Reports are handled confidentially. Depending on what happened, the response
ranges from a private word, to editing or removing a comment, to blocking
the account from the repository.
